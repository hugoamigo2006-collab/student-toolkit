How does the Final Grade Calculator work?

Our Final Grade Calculator helps students determine the score they need on their final exam to reach their desired overall course grade.

Enter your current grade, the percentage of the course that has already been completed, and the final grade you want to achieve.

The calculator then determines the minimum grade you need to earn on your final exam.

Example

If your current grade is 82%, 70% of your course has already been completed, and you want to finish with an 85%, you would need 92% on your final exam.
