How is the required final grade calculated?

Your final course grade is a weighted average of your current grade and your final exam score.

Required Final Exam Score

= (Target Grade − Current Grade × (1 − Final Weight)) ÷ Final Weight

The final exam weight must be converted from a percentage into a decimal when using the formula. For example, a final worth 30% has a weight of 0.30.

Example

Imagine your current course grade is 85%, your final exam is worth 30%, and you want to finish the course with a 90%.

Required score =

(90 − 85 × 0.70) ÷ 0.30

= 101.7%

In this example, the target is not reachable through the final exam alone because even a perfect 100% would not be enough.

Frequently Asked Questions

What grade do I need on my final exam?

Enter your current course grade, the weight of your final exam, and the overall grade you want. The calculator will determine the minimum score you need on the final.

What if I need more than 100%?

If the calculator says you need more than 100%, your target cannot be reached through the final exam alone. Your maximum possible grade is shown below the calculator.

What if I get 100% on my final?

The calculator shows your highest possible overall course grade assuming you score 100% on the final.

What if I get 0% on my final?

The calculator can also estimate your lowest possible final course grade by assuming you receive 0% on the final exam.
