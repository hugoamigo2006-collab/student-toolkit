What is GPA?

GPA stands for Grade Point Average. It is a numerical representation of a student's academic performance.

Many colleges and universities in the United States use a 4.0 GPA scale, although grading systems can vary between institutions.

How is GPA calculated?

GPA is calculated by multiplying each course grade by its credit value, adding those results together, and dividing by the total number of credits.
