How does the Grade Calculator work?

Each assignment contributes to your final course grade according to its percentage weight.

For example, if a midterm is worth 30% of your course and you receive 80%, that midterm contributes 24 points toward your overall grade.

Grade Calculator Formula

A weighted grade is calculated by multiplying each grade by its weight and then adding all the results together.

Overall Grade = (Grade₁ × Weight₁) + (Grade₂ × Weight₂) + (Grade₃ × Weight₃) + ...

Remember that the weights should normally add up to 100% when calculating a complete course grade.

Example

Imagine you have three assessments:

Homework: 90% × 20%

Midterm: 80% × 30%

Project: 95% × 50%

Your weighted course grade would be:

(90 × 0.20) + (80 × 0.30) + (95 × 0.50) = 90.5%

Frequently Asked Questions

What if my weights do not add up to 100%?

If you have not entered every assignment in the course, your weights may be below 100%. The calculator will still calculate the weighted average of the grades you entered.

What if I have many assignments?

Simply use the "Add another assignment" button to add as many assignments as you need.

Does this work for exams and projects?

Yes. You can use the calculator for exams, homework, projects, quizzes, presentations, labs and other graded activities.
