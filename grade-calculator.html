Grade Calculator

Calculate your current course grade using assignment scores and their weights.

Your current course grade will appear here.

What is a weighted grade?

A weighted grade gives different assignments, exams, projects, or activities different levels of importance toward your final course grade.

How does it work?

Enter each assignment's score and its percentage of the total course grade. The calculator combines the weighted scores to determine your current grade.
